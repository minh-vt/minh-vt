I grew up in Tuy Hoa, Phu Yen, and started programming in eighth grade. I had no computer at
home at first, so I went to an internet cafe every day.

In ninth grade I travelled to Hanoi for a summer camp run by FCT Club, a programming club
organised by FPT Software, and stayed with the club for three years until the end of high
school. Nand2Tetris and CS50AI are the two courses I worked through most carefully in that time.

In November 2022 I co-founded Science Lab, a STEM education group for students in Phu Yen, with
friends from Luong Van Chanh High School for the Gifted. From 2023 to 2025 I wrote embedded software at FPT
Software, mostly the image processing for cameras built on Rockchip chips.

I now study IC design at FPT University, work as a teaching assistant at STEAM for Vietnam, and
still run several projects at Science Lab.
