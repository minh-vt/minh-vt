Grew up in Tuy Hoa, Phu Yen. I started programming in the eighth grade. I had no computer at
home for the first stretch, so I went to an internet cafe every day.

In ninth grade I travelled to Hanoi for a summer camp run by FCT Club, a programming club
organised by FPT Software. I stayed with the club for three years, until the end of high school.
Nand2Tetris and CS50AI are the two courses I worked through most carefully in that period.

In November 2022 I started Science Lab with a group of friends from Luong Van Chanh High School
for the Gifted, a non-profit that runs STEM education in Phu Yen. I handle the technical side and
coach the robotics teams.

I study IC design at FPT University and work as a teaching assistant at STEAM for Vietnam.
Outside coursework I work with the robotics teams and read the vLLM source.
